What is Carcharodontosauridae?

Most people like some of the more iconic dinosaurs like Tyrannosaurus rex, Triceratops, Stegosaurus, etc. However, I rather enjoy Giganotosaurus and it's relatives around the world. Giganotosaurus is part of the Carcharodontosauridae family, some of it's relatives are Carcharodontosaurs, Acrocanthosaurus, and Concavenator to name a few. Giganotosaurus has been found in South America and is probably the most popular of the Carcharodontosauridae members since it has been labeled as a "rival" to the very well known Tyrannosaurus rex, and Spinosaurus. Realistically Tyrannosaurus rex would wipe the floor against Giganotosaurus or Spinosaurus in both weight and bite force.

Fossil data

Fossils of Carcharodontosauridae have been found in many different places around the world. Giganotosaurus fossils have been found in Southern Argentina. One of the smaller relatives, Concavenator is found in Spain. Acrocanthosaurus is found in North America specifically around Oklahoma, and Carcharodontosaurs is found in Morocco, Egypt, and Niger to name a few. They all have a similar skull structure which I find very cool to look at. I want to try to find a fossil of any of the members of the Carcharodontosauridae family and possibly even go see more of the fossil remains of some of the other family members in the future.

Appearance in media

The popularity of Carcharodontosauridae has grown a lot in the past couple years with the aid of movies and video games. Specifically for Giganotosaurus, it makes an appearance in Jurassic World Dominion as a somewhat antagonist. With this new popularity a lot of non-dinosaur super nerds have been able to be shown more cool large carnivorous prehistoric apex predators. No matter what someone's favorite dinosaur is, it's a cool dinosaur. I hope more somewhat unpopular species of dinosaurs get the spotlight in the future.
