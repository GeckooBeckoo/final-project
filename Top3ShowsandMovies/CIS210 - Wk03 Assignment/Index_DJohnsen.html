Must watch list (Movies/Series) according to Dane

My top 3 must watch Movies and Series

Jurassic Park

A fantastic movie with amazing animatronic and puppet work to show how these prehistoric beast could possibly act in captivity

Hellsing Ultimate

The art style of this show is what first caught my eye and the story kept me hooked. The action scenes, characters, and twists are cool and all but I like the history aspect of the story.

Police Academy

A fabulous comedy featuring a lot of different types of characters and how they all want to join the police force.
